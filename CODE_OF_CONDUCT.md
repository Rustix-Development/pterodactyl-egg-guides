# Code of Conduct

This is a small community project. We want everyone who helps, asks a question or reports a problem to feel welcome here, whatever their experience.

## Be kind

- Be patient and friendly, especially with people who are new to GitHub or to running servers.
- Give feedback on the guide, not the person. Explain what is wrong and how to fix it.
- Assume good intent. Most mistakes are honest ones.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks or discrimination of any kind.
- Do not use the repo to advertise your hosting company or anything else.

## If something goes wrong

If someone is making this space unpleasant for you or others, please tell the maintainers. Open an issue, or if it is private, contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are taken seriously and kept as private as possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.
